Scoring markers: when a league pays something other than 1.0 per reception, a ▲/▼ badge shows how much that moves a player relative to replacement at his own position — receivers and tight ends lose, pure rushers gain, quarterbacks are untouched. The analysts' order is left alone; the badge is an annotation, not a re-sort. Catch counts are ESTIMATED from position and positional rank with hand overrides, because no provider publishes per-stat projections — treat the number as a direction and a rough size, not a precise figure. Replacement level is derived from the league you point it at — its team count, starting lineup and flex slots — with the flex shared five backs, six receivers, one tight end. A 12-team league with a standard lineup gives RB30, WR31, TE14, QB13; a 10-team league is shallower and a 14-team deeper, and the whole board shifts with it.
